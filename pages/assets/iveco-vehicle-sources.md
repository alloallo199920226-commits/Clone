# IVECO vehicle imagery

Distinct source images are served locally as optimized JPEGs, never hotlinked.
They replace passenger cars and Shineray vehicles without changing product terms.
The same VIP keeps its photo in the catalogue, purchase dialog and purchase history.

Source URL mapping is maintained in `tools/prepare_iveco_vehicle_images.py`.
Relative filenames there use `https://iveco-pts.com/storage/2024/08/`.
Two additional real vehicle photographs come from Truck1's IVECO model pages.
No source screenshot or financial reference image is used as a background.
